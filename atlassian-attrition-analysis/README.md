## Business Problem
In March 2026, Atlassian eliminated 1,600 roles - 10% of its global workforce - citing AI-driven restructuring. Over 900 of those roles were in R&D. This project analyzes real publicly available Atlassian workforce data to identify attrition patterns, quantify the financial cost of turnover, and deliver an AI-augmented predictive risk model with retention recommendations for People Operations leadership.

> Note: This project uses publicly available HR analytics data as a
> structural proxy, applied to Atlassian's publicly documented
> workforce crisis. No proprietary or internal Atlassian data is used.
